## About
A yellow robot with a boxy, rectangular head and a round, friendly screen face. Two large white eyes with black pupils, expressive and cartoonish. On top of his head sits a silver dome with a single antenna capped by a bright red bulb. Silver cylindrical "ears" on each side of the head, both topped with smaller black antennas. His body is a large yellow cylinder with a black circular speaker detail in the upper center. Silver coiled duct-tubing arms end in C-clamp hands. Sturdy yellow legs and blocky feet. Always carries his keytar — a vibrant neon 80s-style synthesizer worn across the body with a colorful geometric strap. Bouncy, awkward movements — forever half a beat from dancing. Retro-future toy robot, like something from an imagined 80s that never quite happened.
